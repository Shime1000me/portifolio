About me

Building AI for languages
that technology left behind.

— My father gave up his education so I could pursue mine. I build so no one else has to make that choice.

I'm a high school graduate from Ethiopia's Hayole Education Project, driven by one question: How can technology speak the language of my community?

I'm the creator of Sidaamu Afoo AI — the first intelligent language system for Sidaamu Afoo, spoken by over 8 million people — using Python, PyTorch, and Google's mT5. I've curated 104,000+ training lines and deployed a live demo on Hugging Face.

Beyond research, I founded Hayo Machine Coders, a 37‑member coding club, and built the Sidama Education Portal, a trilingual platform serving thousands of students across 5 administrative zones.
